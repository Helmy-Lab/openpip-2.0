# Accounts, saving and sharing

Searching and downloading need no account. An account lets you keep networks,
share them with colleagues and discuss them.

## Creating an account

**Register** asks for a username, an email address, a password and three
**security questions**.

- The password must be at least 8 characters. The strength bar is only a
  guide.
- Pick three different questions from the list and answer each. Answers are
  checked without regard to capitalisation or extra spaces.

**Keep your answers.** openPIP sends no email, so the security questions are
the only way to recover your account if you forget your password.

## Signing in and out

Sign in with your username and password. After signing in you return to the
home page, so sign in before you start a search you want to keep.
**Sign out** is on your profile page.

## Forgotten password

1. Choose **Forgot?** on the sign-in page and enter your email address.
2. Answer all three of your security questions.
3. Choose a new password.

Changing your password signs you out on every other device within the hour.
Accounts created before security questions existed cannot use this. Ask the
portal's administrator to reset your password.

## Your profile

**Profile** shows your account. **Edit profile** adds an optional name,
position, affiliation, website, short bio and picture (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP,
up to 2 MB).

**Let colleagues find me by name, lab, or email and share networks with me**
is on by default. Turn it off and other users can no longer find you or send
you networks. Anyone who has your username can still open your profile.

Profiles are visible only to signed-in users. A profile also lists the
networks you and that person have shared with each other.

## Keeping a network

There are two ways to keep a search, and both appear under **Saved Views** on
your profile:

| | Saved view | Saved network (snapshot) |
|---|---|---|
| **What is kept** | The search, its filters, layout, highlight and open result tab | The exact interactions on screen |
| **Opening it** | Runs the search again on the portal's current data | Shows those interactions as they were saved |
| **Can be shared** | Yes, and can have a public link | No |
| **Created by** | **Share Network**, or **Save a copy** of a view shared with you | **Save Network** beside the results |

**Save Network** asks for a name and keeps the interactions currently shown,
so filters and removed proteins apply. It also records the score threshold.

On your profile, each saved view has **Details**, where you can keep private
notes and manage its public link, and **Share**. The **×** on any row deletes
it straight away, without asking.

## Sharing with colleagues

**Share Network**, beside the results, sends what you are looking at to other
users:

1. Give it a name.
2. Find each person by name, username, lab, or their exact email address. You
   can only share with people who have an account and allow sharing.
3. Add an optional note, and choose **Share**.

This saves a view and sends it to each person. They are notified in openPIP;
no email is sent.

Shares appear on your profile under **Shared With Me** and **Shared By Me**.
**Open** shows the network with the sender's filters and layout. In
**Details** or **Discussion**, the two of you can comment, and you can edit
your own comments. Someone who received a view can **Save a copy** to keep it
as their own. The **×** dismisses a share you received, or revokes one you
sent. Either way the discussion is deleted.

## Public links

In a saved view's **Details**, **Create public link** makes an address anyone
can open without an account. They see the network, not your notes. **Turn off
link** stops it working.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar counts unread notifications. You get one when someone
shares a view with you or comments on a share you are part of. The list checks
for new ones every 10 seconds. A short chime plays when something new arrives;
turn it off with **Sound off**. **Mark all read** keeps the list, and **Clear**
empties it.
